## Full description (4000 max)

Fasting, food and strength training — in one clean, ad-free app:

• Fasting timer with 7 science-backed zones — free
• AI meal logging — describe or photograph a meal for instant calories & macros
• Guided strength training — 5 programs, auto progression, form guides
• Full Wear OS app — track fasts and log workouts from your wrist
• Journal & Insights — history, trends and weight tracking
• Private by design — on-device AI, your data stays on your phone

THE FASTING TIMER IS FREE.

Start a fast with one tap and let the live ring guide you through 7 metabolic zones — from Fed State to Deep Renewal — each with practical, science-backed information. Track 16:8, 18:6, 20:4 and more, and get a notification the moment you enter a new zone or reach your goal. No ads, ever.

7 SCIENCE-BACKED FASTING ZONES

• Fed State, Early Fast, Glycogen Burning, Metabolic Switch, Fat Burning, Autophagy and Deep Renewal
• The live ring shows exactly where your body is right now
• Tap any zone to read what's happening inside you and the science behind it
• Milestone notifications that arrive on time

CALORIE & MACRO LOGGING

• Log a meal by describing it in plain words — "2 eggs, a slice of bread and coffee with milk"
• Or snap a photo of your plate — the same on-device AI reads it and fills in the description for you
• On-device AI estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat instantly
• 100% private: both text and photo are processed on your phone, so your food data never leaves the device — no account, no cloud
• See today's intake against your estimated daily burn at a glance
• A quick 7-question Energy Profile (metric or imperial) estimates the calories you burn each day

STRENGTH TRAINING

• 5 proven programs from beginner to advanced — full-body, upper/lower, push-pull-legs and a classic split
• Automatic weight progression and deloads handled for you
• Built-in form guides with illustrations and cues for every exercise
• Log full guided sessions, or quick-log any workout in seconds
• Sessions survive navigation — pause and resume anytime

WEAR OS APP

Track and control everything from your wrist — the watch app runs on its own:

• Start, stop and adjust your fast and see your current zone at a glance
• Log a full strength workout — pick your program, workout and sets right on the watch
• Tile, watch face complications and an ongoing activity that keep your fast one tap away

JOURNAL & INSIGHTS

• Monthly calendar with your complete history — free
• Daily summary: hours fasted and calories eaten at a glance
• Premium Insights: longest fast, averages, calorie and training trends by month, quarter or year
• Weight trend, nutrition patterns and estimated micronutrient coverage

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your data stays on your phone. The meal AI runs fully on-device. No account required.

Meal AI — both text and photo logging — needs a phone with on-device generative AI. Best supported:
• Google Pixel 8, Pixel 9 and newer — Tensor chip with Android AICore
• Samsung Galaxy S25 and S26 series, Z Fold6 / Z Fold7, Z Flip6 / Z Flip7 — on-device Galaxy AI

On other devices you can still log meals in text, and the timer, zones, training and Wear OS features all work as normal.

MORE PREMIUM FEATURES

• Health Connect — fasts, meals and workouts sync both ways with your other health apps
• Custom fasts up to 14 days and custom milestone alerts
• One affordable subscription unlocks everything

WHY HEALTHYFAST?

• Free fasting timer with zero ads
• Private by design — your data stays on your phone
• Built for Android: Material You design, Wear OS and Health Connect
• Fasting, food and training in one app instead of three

Download HealthyFast and make your next fast — and your next workout — your best one.

Note: HealthyFast is a wellness and education tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting any fasting or exercise routine, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or take medication.
