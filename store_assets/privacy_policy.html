Privacy Policy for HealthyFast

Last updated: 6 August 2026

HealthyFast ("the app") is developed by Northern App Dev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how. By default HealthyFast keeps your data on your own device — no account needed for the free fasting timer. Two Premium features send data to Google Cloud and require signing in with your Google account: Cloud AI (sharper meal and program estimates) and Cloud Backup (keeps a copy of your history tied to your Google account). Both require your explicit consent and are described below.

Data the app stores on your device

HealthyFast stores the following information locally on your phone or watch so the app can work:

  • Your fasting sessions (start time, end time, chosen protocol and goal).
  • Meals you log (description, calories, and macronutrients you enter or that are estimated).
  • Your app settings and premium entitlement status.

This data stays on your device unless you turn on Cloud AI or Cloud Backup below. We do not access it otherwise.

AI meal estimation

By default, when you ask the app to estimate a meal's or workout program's content, the description or photo is processed by an on-device AI model (Google's Gemini Nano via Android ML Kit) entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else for this.

On phones that don't support the on-device model, or if you turn on "Always use cloud AI" (Premium), the same request is instead sent to Google's Gemini model through a Cloud Function we operate — only after you've explicitly consented in-app and signed in with your Google account. We require sign-in (not just anonymous access) purely to stop the shared daily AI quota from being abused by scripted, account-less requests; it's the same account already used for Cloud Backup if you have that enabled. We do not store your meal descriptions or photos on our servers: they are forwarded to Google's Vertex AI to generate a response, which is returned to your device and then discarded server-side. We do keep a daily usage count tied to your account, used only for this rate-limiting. You can turn Cloud AI off, or sign out, at any time in Settings → Cloud & AI.

Health Connect

If you choose to enable it, HealthyFast can write your completed fasts and logged meals to Android Health Connect, so other health apps you use can access them. This is optional and requires your explicit permission. You can revoke this permission at any time in Health Connect or your device settings. We do not read data from Health Connect beyond what is needed to confirm and write these records, and we do not transmit Health Connect data to our own servers.

Wear OS companion

If you use the Wear OS version, your fasting state is synchronised between your own phone and your own paired watch using Google's Wearable Data Layer. This data travels only between your devices.

Purchases

HealthyFast offers a one-time purchase and a subscription, processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details; these are handled by Google. The app stores only whether your premium access is active.

Cloud backup (Premium)

If you're a Premium subscriber and turn on Cloud Backup, HealthyFast signs you in with your Google account and uploads your fasting sessions, logged meals, weight entries and workouts to Google Firestore, so you can restore them on a new device. This is off by default and only starts once you explicitly enable it in Settings → Cloud & AI.

Turning the Cloud Backup switch off disconnects your account but does not delete data already uploaded. To permanently delete it, use "Delete cloud backup data" in Settings → Cloud & AI — this removes your fasts, meals, weights and workouts from our servers immediately and cannot be undone. Data stored locally on your device is never affected by this.

Data retention and deletion

Local data: Your fasting sessions, logged meals, settings, and premium status stay stored locally for as long as the app is installed. We never receive a copy of this unless you turn on Cloud AI or Cloud Backup above, so there is no additional retention period on our side for local-only data.

Cloud AI: We don't store your meal descriptions or photos — see "AI meal estimation" above for exactly what is and isn't kept.

Cloud Backup: Uploaded records are retained until you delete them (see "Cloud backup" above) or contact us to request deletion on your behalf.

Deletion: You can delete your data at any time:

  • Uninstall the app — this immediately and permanently deletes all HealthyFast data stored on your device.
  • Clear app data without uninstalling, via Android Settings → Apps → HealthyFast → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
  • Delete individual records written to Health Connect via the Health Connect app (Health Connect → Data and access → HealthyFast).
  • Delete your cloud backup at any time via Settings → Cloud & AI → Delete cloud backup data.

If you need help with any of this, or want us to remove the Cloud AI usage counter tied to your account, contact us at the email below.

Device information

The app reads basic device characteristics locally (for example, whether it is running on a watch) to show the correct interface. This information is not collected or shared.

Children

HealthyFast is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy
