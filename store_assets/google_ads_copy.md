# Google Ads copy — uniqueness angle

## "Describe what's unique about your business" (max 3000 chars)

For the free-text business description field Google uses to generate
campaign suggestions. 2,980 characters — right up against the limit
without going over.

```
HealthyFast is the only health app that combines intermittent fasting, AI-powered meal logging, and structured strength training in one clean, ad-free product — most competitors do just one of these well; HealthyFast does all three, and treats privacy as core to the product rather than a footnote.

Fasting: a live timer guides users through 7 science-backed metabolic zones, from Fed State to Deep Renewal, each explained in plain language along with the science behind it. Notifications arrive the moment a user enters a new zone or hits their goal. Supports 16:8, 18:6, 20:4 and fully custom fasting windows up to 14 days. The fasting timer and all seven zones are completely free, with zero ads — not a trial or a teaser feature.

Meal logging: users describe a meal in plain words or snap a photo of their plate, and AI estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds. On supported phones this runs entirely on-device using Google's Gemini Nano — no account, no cloud, food data never leaves the phone. On older phones without that hardware, an opt-in cloud AI fallback extends the same text- and photo-based meal logging to a much wider range of devices, so it isn't limited to flagship phones only.

Strength training: 5 structured programs from beginner to advanced — full-body, upper/lower, push-pull-legs and a classic split — with automatic weight progression, deloads, and built-in form guides for every exercise. Users can run a fully guided session or quick-log any workout in seconds, and sessions survive interruptions, so they can pause and resume anytime.

Wear OS: a full companion watch app lets users start and stop a fast, see their current metabolic zone at a glance, and log an entire strength workout — exercise, sets and reps — directly from their wrist, with a tile and watch-face complications keeping their fast visible without opening the phone.

Journal & Insights turn daily logging into trends over time: hours fasted, calories eaten, longest fasts, weight and training volume by month, quarter or year, with Health Connect sync into whatever other health apps a user already relies on.

Built specifically for Android, not ported from iOS — Material You design, deep Wear OS integration and Health Connect support are first-class. Maintained by a small, independent team, which shows in how tightly scoped and privacy-first the product stays.

Target users: people who've outgrown single-purpose fasting timers or generic calorie counters and want fasting, nutrition and strength training handled by one serious, private tool instead of three separate apps — and anyone drawn to on-device AI and data privacy over cloud-based food-tracking apps that require an account and upload photos to a server.

Business model: the fasting timer and its 7 zones are free, with zero ads, for every user. A single subscription (monthly or yearly) unlocks AI meal logging, strength training, Wear OS sync, Health Connect integration and deeper insights.
```

## Why these lean on uniqueness

Most fasting apps don't do strength training, and most calorie/macro
trackers don't do fasting zones — HealthyFast doing fasting, food, and
lifting in one ad-free app is the core differentiator, so most lines lead
with that combination or with the on-device AI privacy angle (data never
leaving the phone), since that's a real point of difference from
cloud-based meal-scanning competitors.
